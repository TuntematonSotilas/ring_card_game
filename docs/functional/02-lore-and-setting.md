# Lore & setting

> The source material and how it maps onto game content (factions, characters, artifacts, campaign).
> Status: Draft · Last updated: 2026-10-09

## Source material

*Der Ring des Nibelungen* — four music dramas by Richard Wagner (premiered as a cycle in 1876). The story is drawn from Norse and Germanic myth (Eddas, *Völsunga saga*, *Nibelungenlied*). The libretto and score are in the public domain.

## The four operas → campaign chapters

| # | Opera | Story summary | Chapter focus |
|---|-------|---------------|---------------|
| 0 | *Das Rheingold* | Alberich steals the Rhinegold and forges the Ring; Wotan tricks him to pay the Giants for Valhalla; the Ring is cursed. | Tutorial + Gods vs Nibelungs |
| 1 | *Die Walküre* | Siegmund and Sieglinde; Brünnhilde defies Wotan and is put to sleep in a ring of fire. | Wälsungs, Valkyries |
| 2 | *Siegfried* | Siegfried reforges Nothung, slays Fafner the dragon, wins the Ring, wakes Brünnhilde. | Heroes vs Giants/dragon |
| 3 | *Götterdämmerung* | Hagen's plot, Siegfried's death, Brünnhilde returns the Ring to the Rhine; Valhalla burns. | Gibichungs, final battle |

## Factions (proposal)

| Faction | Members | Play style (draft) |
|---------|---------|--------------------|
> **Decision (2026-10-09):** 4 playable factions at launch. Giants and Gibichungs are not playable factions in v1.0: their characters are **Neutral** cards and campaign opponents (candidates for a later expansion).

| Faction | Members | Play style (draft) |
|---------|---------|--------------------|
| **Gods (Æsir)** | Wotan, Fricka, Donner, Froh, Freia, Loge | Control, contracts/pacts, powerful but bound by law |
| **Nibelungs** | Alberich, Mime, Nibelung dwarves | Gold economy, forging artifacts, swarm |
| **Wälsungs & Heroes** | Siegmund, Sieglinde, Siegfried | Aggression, growing units, weapon synergies |
| **Valkyries** | Brünnhilde, the 8 sisters | Resurrecting fallen units, flying |
| *Neutral* | Rhinemaidens, Norns, Erda, Woodbird · Giants (Fasolt, Fafner) · Gibichungs (Gunther, Gutrune, Hagen) | Utility, fate / foresight, big Giant units, Gibichung deception |

## Key artifacts (card candidates)

- **The Ring** — unique legendary; huge power, carries the curse.
- **The Rhinegold** — resource / win-condition theme.
- **Tarnhelm** — invisibility / transformation.
- **Nothung** — sword, broken then reforged (two-state card).
- **Wotan's spear** — contracts carved on it; enforces rules.
- **Potion of forgetfulness** — mind control / steal.
- **Magic fire** — protective barrier around a unit.

## Tone

Epic, tragic, mythic. Visual and textual tone takes the opera seriously (no parody). Card flavour texts can quote or paraphrase the libretto (public domain; use our own English translation or a public-domain one).

> **Decision (2026-10-09):** Original German names in every language (Wotan, Brünnhilde, Nothung, Götterdämmerung…). Only UI and rules text are translated.
